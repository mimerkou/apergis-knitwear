This Personal Data Protection Policy describes how the personal data processed by AFOI APERGI O.E. are collected, used and protected.

01Types of personal data and the purpose of their processing by AFOI APERGI O.E.

AFOI APERGI O.E. collects and generally processes your personal data for marketing purposes, including sending newsletters. Processing your personal data allows us to contact you by post, email, phone calls or text messages (SMS). The personal data we collect for these purposes include general identification and contact details, such as your full name, your address, your email address and your phone number.

02Legal basis for using your personal data

We collect, use and disclose your personal data where:

  • you have given your consent, or
  • the processing of your personal data is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by AFOI APERGI O.E., which override your interests or your fundamental rights and freedoms that require protection of your personal data.

In addition, by law and without your consent being required, we are entitled to use: (a) personal data we have collected from publicly accessible sources to contact you by phone for the above processing purpose, after confirming that you have not told your telecommunications provider that you generally do not wish to receive such calls, or (b) your email contact details, lawfully obtained in the context of a sale of products or services or another transaction between you and AFOI APERGI O.E., to fulfil the above processing purpose.

03Transfer of your personal data

We do not share personal data with third parties other than: (a) third-party companies acting on behalf of AFOI APERGI O.E. as processors, under a specific confidentiality and data protection agreement we have concluded with them, and (b) third parties to whom AFOI APERGI O.E. is required by law to transfer your data.

04Your rights

With regard to your personal data, you have the following rights:

  • to access and rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to their processing, and the right to data portability,
  • to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, and
  • to withdraw your consent to the processing of your data for the purpose of this Policy.

05Retention period

Your personal data will be kept for as long as permitted or required by the legislation in force. Where we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, we will continue to process them until you withdraw it.

06Contact

If you have any questions about this Policy or wish to exercise your rights regarding the protection of your personal data, please contact the Data Protection Officer of AFOI APERGI O.E. at apergisknitwear@gmail.com
