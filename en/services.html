Manufacturing services

From design to delivery.

We manufacture knitwear for fashion businesses and professionals in our workshop in Nea Ionia, Athens. From knitting to packing, to your design and specifications.

The stages

Five stages, one garment.

We take on production as a whole. Tell us what your design needs and we will suggest how to proceed.

  1. 01Knitting
  2. 02Cutting
  3. 03Linking & sewing
  4. 04Pressing & finishing
  5. 05Packing
01

Knitting

We knit the garment panels on Steiger flat knitting machines, following your design and tech sheet.

  • 5, 7 and 12 gauge
  • Single jersey, rib and cables
  • Jacquard, intarsia and textured structures
Knitting capabilities
02

Cutting

We cut the knitted panels precisely, so the garment keeps the shape and proportions of the design.

03

Linking & sewing

In our sewing room, equipped with sewing machines, we join the panels into a finished garment. Sewing is what we are known for.

  • Joining the knitted panels
  • Clean seams and trims
04

Pressing & finishing

In finishing we soften the knit with different washing methods and set it with steam. After pressing, every piece leaves ready.

  • Washing and softening
  • Steam setting
  • Pressing
05

Packing

Folding and packing the garments, ready for shipping.

Knitting capabilities

From chunky to fine knit.

We knit in 5, 7 and 12 gauge, that is 5, 7 or 12 needles per inch. The higher the number, the finer and denser the knit.

Knit structures

  • Single jersey
  • Rib
  • Cables
  • Jacquard
  • Intarsia
  • Textured structures

Working together

What we need, what you get.

The more complete your request, the faster and more accurately we can reply.

You send us

  • A sketch or tech sheetor a reference garment, if you have one
  • Yarn, colours and compositionor the texture you want to achieve
  • Quantities and sizes
  • When you need the production

We send back

  • A proposal for gauge and knit structure
  • A production quote
  • Samplinga sample of the garment for review and approval
  • An estimated delivery time

Quantities and lead times. Minimum quantities and lead times depend on the design, the yarn and the gauge. We discuss them with you along with the quote.
