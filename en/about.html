Our story

In 1982 the Apergis family founded its knitwear workshop in Nea Ionia, Athens. From the start, what set us apart was the sewing and the quality of the garments that left our workshop.

We produce high-quality knitwear, mainly womenswear, in contemporary designs and colours. Over the years we have worked with major Greek fashion companies and with companies abroad.

Today we produce knitwear for fashion businesses and professionals, in the same workshop in Nea Ionia.

The business is still family-run: today the next generation carries it on.
